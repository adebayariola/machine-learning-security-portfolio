# Student Lifestyle Dashboard

## Data
Student lifestyle dataset; 2,000 records

## Tools
Excel, data validation, PivotTables and charts

## Work completed
- Checked missing values and duplicate identifiers and summarised counts, percentages and average study and sleep hours by stress level.
- Built dashboard views to compare student lifestyle patterns and explain the findings visually.

## Evidence and limitations
No date timeline is attributed to this undated dataset.

Raw datasets and private operational records are not distributed.
