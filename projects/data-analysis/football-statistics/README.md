## Research question

How do match statistics relate to football results?

## Dataset

Manchester United 2023/24 Premier League match records; 30 matches

## Tools and methods

R, R Markdown, statistical tests and regression

## What I implemented

- Produced descriptive statistics and visualisations of possession, home/away performance, shots, corners, goals and outcomes.
- Applied probability calculations, hypothesis tests and linear regression to explore match-performance relationships.
