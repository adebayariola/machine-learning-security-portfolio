# Retail Product and Customer Review Analysis

## Data
JC Penney products.csv, reviews.csv, users.csv, jcpenney_products.json and jcpenney_reviewers.json

## Tools
Python, pandas, TextBlob, JSON/CSV handling, image processing and Google Colab

## Work completed
- Cleaned and joined product, review and customer records, then analysed pricing, ratings and customer geography.
- Combined review-text polarity with numerical ratings, flagged disagreements and added product-image rotation, flipping and brightness augmentation with error logging.

## Evidence and limitations
Product data and coursework customer records have different provenance; no claim is made that every record represents an observed customer.

Raw datasets and private operational records are not distributed.

This entry documents the project; source code is not included in this publication.
